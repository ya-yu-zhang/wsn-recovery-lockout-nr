Figure captions (LaTeX source text), in order of appearance; Figure numbers as printed: Fig1..Fig6 in the article, FigS1 in the supplement.

Fig1 (fig:1):
Recovery-gate state machine aligned with Proposition~1. Solid edges: implemented Joint confirmation contract; S4/S5 are reached after an unconfirmed exit ($T=\mathrm{Unconfirmed}$), which in this paper arises either from natural incomplete install delivery or from a disclosed controlled gateway timedrop (Amplification-2)---not depicted here as spontaneous wireless loss. S5 records \texttt{installation\_unconfirmed} with all $F_s$ ineligible ($\Phi=0$). Green edges: Joint exits from S3--S5 (late confirmation, eligible $F_s$, or $\Psi$ = Poor). Dashed blue: PostExit ablation arm releasing only premise $T$ from S5 (Sections~\ref{sec:ablation} and~\ref{sec:amp2}); S6 is reached only in that arm. Observation repairs that leave $T$ and $\{F_s\}$ unchanged (Horizon, Cohort, Closing) are self-loops on S5. $\Phi$: authorization; $\Psi$: service predicate; IU: installation unconfirmed. Drawn programmatically from the frozen records by the figure script described in Section~\ref{sec:vv-reg}.

Fig2 (fig:2):
Paired service and control-DATA effects relative to Legacy for four matched cells per network size. \textbf{(a, c)} Worst-source service change; \textbf{(b, d)} control-DATA reduction; \textbf{(a, b)} ten nodes; \textbf{(c, d)} twenty nodes. Circles denote relay-removal workloads, squares denote overload, filled/open markers distinguish the two shared input seeds, and vertical bars give four-cell means. Dotted lines mark the frozen screening criteria; grey shading marks values below the service criterion (service change $\le -0.5$ percentage points) or below the DATA criterion (reduction $<20\%$). Shading marks failure of a screening criterion, not lockout. Drawn programmatically from the frozen records by the figure script described in Section~\ref{sec:vv-reg}.

Fig3 (fig:3):
Confirmation-contract lockout in the selected ten-node adverse cell (companion to Supplementary Table~S3). \textbf{(a)} Schematic topology after relay 6 removal at 225~s (not physical coordinates), where source 0 remains on the broken route (v2), and source 1 holds the intact route (v3). \textbf{(b)} Decision lockout timeline including admitted yet channel-failed installation attempts, unconfirmed exit at 245~s, and the 15 blocked recovery checks during 245--315~s; boxes summarize the check outcomes per model. The diamond at 265~s marks the offline received-only audit, which identifies a historical deficit without authorizing recovery (no action). In (a), the dashed grey route with crosses is broken and the solid route intact; arm colours of the other figures are not used here. Drawn programmatically from the frozen records by the figure script described in Section~\ref{sec:vv-reg}.

Fig4 (fig:vv):
Multi-level V\&V protocol as applied in this study. Each lane compares the ns-3.44 packet-level reference model, or its frozen Amplification-2 records (registration SHA256 1f37be33\ldots), with a second model or a measured input, against an endpoint registered before the runs; the verdict and the claim it licenses or restricts are shown on the right (details in Box~1 and Table~\ref{tab:10}). Symbols: check mark, registered criterion met; cross, not met; slashed circle, hypothesis declared void before the runs. Drawn programmatically from the frozen records by the figure script described in Section~\ref{sec:vv-reg}.

Fig5 (fig:4):
Trace-driven replay over measured FlockLab links (192 runs). Fractions of runs with at least one unconfirmed exit, IU-only E1 lockout to the action deadline, and an installation after the exit; points are proportions of 16 runs with Wilson 95\% intervals, and the counts $k/n$ are printed at the right. Open markers denote $0/16$ (Wilson upper limit 0.19, drawn). \textbf{(a)} Scenario N (relay faults, no install drop). \textbf{(b)} Scenario M1 (frozen Amplification-2 recipe). Joint: vermilion circles; PostExit: blue squares. Days A/B are the two measurement days; R2 is bitstream replay and R1 Bernoulli replay at the day reception ratio ($^{\dagger}$: R1 is conditional on the failed ingestion audit). Outcomes are exploratory. Drawn programmatically from the frozen records by the figure script described in Section~\ref{sec:vv-reg}.

Each cell gives the Joint E1 count out of 8 runs (bold), the number of those runs with an unconfirmed exit, and the relay-fault level and install wait aliased with that layout in the design (early faults: \texttt{[[100,3],[180,6]]}; late faults: \texttt{[[145,3],[225,6]]}). Colour scale: cividis. Drawn programmatically from the frozen records by the figure script described in Section~\ref{sec:vv-reg}.
